chatzip

Turn your AI data exports into one tidy zip: a folder per AI, a text file per chat. Everything happens in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Pick the export files you downloaded from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek (.zip or .json). You can pick several at once.

Getting your exports

  • Each AI has a "export data" option in its settings; it sends you a file to download. This page cannot log in to your accounts or fetch chats for you.
  • Gemini: use Google Takeout, select My Activity > Gemini Apps, and switch the format from HTML to JSON. Google does not group prompts into chats, so you get one file per day.
  • Claude and ChatGPT are tested. Gemini, Grok and DeepSeek support is based on their documented formats and may need fixes.
